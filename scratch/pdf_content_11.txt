Abstract. Small Language Models (SLMs) offer potential for privacy-
preserving clinical decision support but frequently exhibit domain-specific
hallucinations in resource-scarce languages like Vietnamese—such as mis-
calculating pediatric dosages or violating pregnancy contraindications.
We propose Early-Stopping Activation Steering, a framework evaluat-
ing temporally bounded activation steering on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct at
Layer 8 with Hard Cutoff and Linear Decay schedules restricting vec-
tor injection to the firstK=16decoding steps (t∈ [1, 16], influencing

1 Introduction
Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated capabilities across healthcare
tasks [3]. However, in specialized domains requiring high factual precision—such
as clinical pharmacology and prescription guidance in non-English languages—
standard models suffer from domain-specific hallucinations [7]. In Vietnamese
clinical settings, these hallucinations range from benign stylistic errors to danger-
ous medical contradictions, such as recommending contraindicated medications
during pregnancy or miscalculating weight-based pediatric dosages.

Traditional mitigation strategies rely on post-hoc supervised fine-tuning (SFT)
[10] or Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) [5]. Although RAG provides
external knowledge contexts, models may ignore retrieved evidence when internal
activation pathways favor non-truthful generation [11], additionally incurring
heavy context window overheads and retrieval latencies. Supervised fine-tuning
demands substantial computational resources and risks catastrophic forgetting of
general abilities [12].
Recently, Representation Engineering (RepE) [1] and Inference-Time Inter-
vention (ITI) [2] have emerged as lightweight, non-destructive alternatives. By
extracting a truthfulness steering vectorvsteer from hidden activation contrasts
and injecting it into intermediate layers during generation, activation steering
shifts model outputs toward factual accuracy without modifying underlying
weights. Related approaches such as Activation Addition (ActAdd) [8] and con-
trastive activation addition [9] have demonstrated the viability of linear steering
for behavioral control.
However, continuous activation steering (K = ∞) induces persistent hidden-
state norm elevation over long output sequences, associated with cumulative
activation displacement and text repetition. In this paper, we presentEarly-
Stopping Activation Steering, a framework evaluating temporally bounded
intervention via Hard Cutoff and Linear Decay schedules restricting vector
injection to the first K = 16decoding steps ( t∈ [1, 16], influencing tokens
y2 . . . y17).

2 Related Work
2.1 Hallucination in Medical Language Models
LLM hallucinations in clinical domains pose unique risks compared to general-
purpose applications. Ji et al. [7] survey hallucination phenomena, categorizing
them into intrinsic and extrinsic types. In medical settings, Singhal et al. [3]
showed that while large models encode clinical knowledge, they remain prone to
factual errors in drug interactions, dosage calculations, and contraindications. The
HaluEval benchmark [13] introduced evaluation across diverse domains, inspiring
our domain-specific ViHaluEval-Medical benchmark for Vietnamese clinical texts.
Lin et al. [16] proposed TruthfulQA for measuring model truthfulness, establishing
evaluation protocols that informed our contrastive design.
2.2 Inference-Time Intervention and Representation Engineering
Rather than modifying model weights through parameter-efficient fine-tuning
[10], inference-time methods intervene directly in internal representations during
generation. Li et al. [2] proposed ITI, identifying truthful directions in attention
head outputs. Zou et al. [1] generalized this to Representation Engineering
(RepE), demonstrating that high-level concepts can be extracted and controlled
via linear subspace manipulation. Turner et al. [8] introduced ActAdd, showing
that simple vector addition to residual streams steers model behavior. Rimsky et
al. [9] extended this with contrastive activation addition. Our work builds upon
these foundations but addresses the limitation of continuous steering over long
sequences by comparing Hard Cutoff and Linear Decay schedules for temporally
bounded steering.
2.3 Evaluation Metrics for Clinical Text Generation
Traditional n-gram overlap metrics like ROUGE [14] provide lexical similarity
scores but fail to capture semantic equivalence in multilingual clinical settings.
Zhang et al. [15] proposed BERTScore, which computes token-level cosine simi-
larities using contextual BERT embeddings, offering evaluation robust to lexical
variation. We adopt a BERTScore-based reference-preference criterion as a proxy
for factual preference alignment.

Exact Hook Location, Insertion & PyTorch Code.Steering hook is
attached via PyTorchregister_forward_hook directly to residual output of
decoder block model.model.layers[8] (index 8, 9th block of 28-layer archi-
tecture). During initial prompt prefill (t = 0, input sequence length> 1), the
hook executes unsteered and initializes counterself.t = 0. During single-token
autoregressive decoding (t≥ 1, tensor sequence dimension equals 1), counter
increments self.t += 1, KV cache is preserved via PyTorch’s native dynamic
caching mechanism, and steering vectorvsteer is dynamically injected into the fi-
nal sequence positionout_tensor[:, -1, :]. Sequence termination is triggered
naturally upon emitting the end-of-sequence token (tokenizer.eos_token_id).
PyTorch hook implementation:
class SteeringHookHandler:
def __init__(self, v_steer, alpha_0=18.0, K=16, schedule="linear_decay"):
self.v_steer, self.alpha_0, self.K, self.schedule, self.t = v_steer, alpha_0, K,
schedule, 0
def reset(self): self.t = 0
def hook_fn(self, module, input, output):
out = output[0] if isinstance(output, tuple) else output
if out.shape[1] > 1: self.t = 0; return output
self.t += 1
a_t = self.alpha_0 * max(0.0, 1.0 - (self.t-1)/self.K) if self.schedule == "
linear_decay" else (self.alpha_0 if self.t <= self.K else 0.0) if self.schedule
== "hard_cutoff" else (self.alpha_0 if self.schedule == "continuous" else 0.0)
if a_t != 0.0: out[:, -1, :] += a_t * self.v_steer.to(device=out.device, dtype=out.
dtype)
return output

5 Discussion & Limitations
Methodological Scope, Proxy Metrics & Cumulative Dose.Reference-
preference accuracy is a semantic proxy for factual alignment rather than a
clinician-adjudicated correctness rate; validation against blinded clinician labels
is required in future work. Equal initialα0 produces unequal cumulative doses
across schedules (Section 3.3). While Nominal Matched-Dose control sets a
constant per-token coefficientαmatch = 0.0425α0 (0.7650for α0 = 18.0), executed
dose scales with post-prefill sequence length (Dexecuted = (Tgen−1)|αmatch|). Fine-
grained schedule differences remain small and should be treated as comparative
ablations.
HyperparameterValidation&Activation-NormDynamics.Validation
tuning (Neval = 500) identified peak performance at Layer 8 (77.40%, vs74 .20%–
76.80%for layers 4–14), cutoffK = 16(77.40%, vs74.80%–76.80%for K = 4–32),
and scale α0 = 18.0(77 .40%, vs72 .20%–76.80%for α0 = 5–25). Extending
steering pastK = 16accumulated active norm drift during injection (K = 24:
∆(20)
norm = +1.12at t = 20; K = 32: ∆(20)
norm = +1.98). Deactivating vector
injection past stepK directly restores Layer 8 outputs to unsteered baseline
magnitude (53.39 ± 0.64, ∆(50)
norm = 0.00), preventing persistent norm elevation
(∆ = +2.95under continuous steering). Downstream residual propagation past
Layer 8 remains a structural hypothesis.
Statistical Validation, Placebo Controls & Natural Termination.Per-
centile bootstrap CIs (B = 10, 000) use paired question-index resampling across
identical test item indices (i∈ [1, 500]). Isotropic placebo controls (N = 100,
55.82% ± 4.15%, max64 .60%) confirm+ vsteer (77.20%) achieves an upper-
tail rank ofr≥ = 0(1 /101empirical rank, pemp = 0.0099) at+5 .15σ separa-
tion, presented as a descriptive Monte Carlo comparison against isotropic noise.
Covariance-matched controls (74.81% ± 0.95%) decouple general subspace covari-
ance effects (+18.99 pp over isotropic noise) from directional reference-preference
alignment (+2.39 pp over covariance mean). Under high-cap natural comple-
tion (max_new_tokens=800), Hard Cutoff (K = 16) maintains 100.00% EOS hit
rate while improving preference accuracy (+5.20 pp over baseline: 70.60% vs
65.40%, exact binomial McNemarp = 0.00086, padj = 0.0026, 95% Bootstrap CI
[+2.20pp,+8.20pp]).
6 Conclusion
We introduced Early-Stopping Activation Steering, a temporally bounded
inference-time intervention for Vietnamese clinical Small Language Models.
Evaluated on ViHaluEval-Medical (14,674 records derived from the Vietnamese
National Drug Formulary, 2nd Edition, 2018), restricting steering to initial
